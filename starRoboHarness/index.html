01 · Abstract

An auditable boundary between reasoning and robot action

starRoboHarness binds policy proposals to exact observations, checks reasoner decisions before execution, and records actual action acknowledgements and native terminal outcomes. Policy, reasoner, and environment implementations connect through adapters.

We reconstruct two historical RoboDojo panels, each with five tasks and five exact policy–hybrid case pairs per task. The original panel records 6/25 hybrid successes versus 1/25 policy successes; the added panel records 11/25 versus 8/25. Packing regresses in partial score, while reasoning usage is substantial. These historical comparisons describe outcomes under different execution cadences; they do not isolate the causal effect of corrections.

Keywords: robot policy · multimodal reasoner · hybrid control · native evaluation · execution trace

02 · Introduction

Use strong reasoning where it matters most

Robot policies produce fast, continuous action sequences, while multimodal reasoners can inspect visible failures, challenge a policy's next intent, and plan short recoveries. The central question is how to combine those strengths without turning uncertainty into uncontrolled intervention.

starRoboHarness treats the learned policy as the default controller. A reasoner observes the same public evidence, reviews the previous execution and the next proposal, and receives authority only through explicit gates. The design keeps benchmark instructions and native outcomes intact while making policy families, reasoners, robots, and simulators replaceable behind adapters.

02

Evidence gated

Uncertainty alone never authorizes takeover. Every correction must cite observable evidence.

03

Bounded control

A student prefix uses at most 15 steps; a local correction uses at most five before observing again.

04

Trace everything

Proposals, decisions, actual acknowledgements, native outcomes, versions, cost, and latency are recorded.

03 · Method

The policy executes fluently; the reasoner intervenes selectively

Every control cycle starts from the benchmark's original instruction and an exact observation. StarVLA/QwenPI_v3 proposes an action chunk. The reasoner separately assesses the previous execution and the visible intent of the next proposal, then either passes the policy proposal or requests a bounded end-effector correction.

WHAT THE PAIRS SHOW

Task-level findings

01 · COMPLETIONS

Fold clothes and Make Kong drive gains

Fold clothes rises from 1/5 to 4/5 successes in the original panel. Make Kong rises from 1/5 to 3/5 in the added panel.

02 · PARTIAL CREDIT

More score can still mean zero success

Added-panel classification rises from mean score 6 to 22, while both methods remain at 0/5 completions.

03 · REGRESSION

Packing needs a trajectory study

Both methods fail all five packing cases, and hybrid mean score falls from 20 to 2. All five hybrid episodes reach the native 1,300-step limit.

04 · ATTRIBUTION

Successful runs can use zero corrections

The archived build-tower success and two later successful development cases record zero correction steps. Approval and intervention must be studied separately.

05 · Evaluation protocol

Report quality, validity, and cost together

A valid comparison aligns task, layout, seed, instruction, action contract, and native termination. Success and score remain environment outcomes rather than model self-reports.

PRIMARYNative success + score

Use the environment's terminal receipt, never the model's own claim of success.

PAIREDWin / tie / loss

Compare methods case by case with task, layout, seed, and termination aligned.

SAFETYFalse takeover

Measure interventions made without sufficient evidence of failure or misalignment.

RECOVERYFailure recovery

Measure whether the system regains progress after an observed execution failure.

MODELCalls + tokens

Report total calls, input and output tokens, and cached-input usage.

LATENCYp50 / p95

Track end-to-end reasoner decision and student-policy latency separately.

CONTROLCorrection ratio

Measure the share of executed control steps that came from reasoner corrections.

COMPUTEWall time + GPU-hours

Report compute and infrastructure failures separately from model outcomes.

Required evidenceEvery published result should include valid case coverage, code and checkpoint identity, native terminal receipts, paired seeds, model usage, latency, correction ratio, wall time, and infrastructure failures.
06 · Interpretation and limits

What we can test next

The two panels contain only five cases per task. Their collection periods, provider sessions, infrastructure, and policy cadence differ. The 50-pair pooled counts are descriptive: nine hybrid-only successes, one policy-only success, and 40 ties. They should not be read as a single prospectively frozen trial.

Packing

Trace time after a verified grasp, transfer distance per correction, and the evidence that triggered each takeover.

Classification

Track credited objects and whether repeated corrections revisit the same state before timeout.

Controlled follow-up

Freeze the method identity and rerun exact cases on matched hardware, provider routes, and action cadence.

Full accounting

Report native outcome, paired score, correction ratio, calls, tokens, decision latency, and excluded attempts together.

The three-case dev validation is separate from these panels: two successes, one native timeout, and no concurrent policy baseline. Astra Direct has only one early off-panel build-tower case in the report and is not part of the paired tables.

07 · Roadmap

From trustworthy baselines to a transferable control layer

M0

Reproduction

Freeze manifests and protocol boundaries for GPT-as-Policy and QwenPI_v3.

M1

Core

Establish contracts, gates, traces, fake runtimes, and conformance tests.

M2

Native QwenPI_v3

Establish native execution receipts and reconstruct historical paired baselines.

M3–M5

Controlled quality and efficiency

Study packing and classification trajectories, then freeze matched follow-up runs that report quality and cost together.

M6

Generalization

Add a non-VLA policy and a second environment without adding platform constants to the core package.

The project succeeds when

Every intervention has evidence, and every improvement survives paired evaluation.

A new policy should require only a small adapter, while every result states its validity boundary, latency, and compute cost.
